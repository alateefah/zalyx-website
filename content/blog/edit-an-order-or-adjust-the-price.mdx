---
title: Edit the order or adjust the price? How to correct a sale the right way
summary: Two ways to fix a sale in Zalyx, and the one question that tells you which to use.
published: 2026-10-07
author: Lateefah Abdulkareem
---

A customer ordered two shirts for ₦10,000. You wrote it down as one shirt for
₦5,000. Or the customer paid in full last week and today asks for a discount.
Both are corrections, and they are not the same job.

Zalyx gives you two tools for this, and which one you use depends on a single
question: **has the customer finished paying?**

## Not fully paid yet? Edit the order

While an order is unpaid or only part-paid, it is still open. You can change
what is on it: the items, the quantity, the price, the due date.

Use **Edit order** when the order was recorded wrong in the first place:

- you picked the wrong item
- the quantity or price is wrong
- the customer added or removed something before paying

Open the order, tap the menu at the top right, and choose **Edit order**. The
total and the balance update straight away, and the order shows the corrected
version.

## Fully paid? Adjust the price

Once an order is paid in full, it is closed. That is on purpose: a paid sale is
a record of something that happened, and you should not be able to quietly
rewrite it.

So a paid order has no **Edit order**. Instead you choose **Adjust price**,
which adds a change on top of the original:

- **A surcharge**, for example ₦1,500 for delivery you agreed afterwards. The
  customer now owes ₦1,500 and the order reopens until they pay it.
- **A discount or refund**, for example ₦2,000 back because one item was
  damaged. Zalyx records the ₦2,000 going back to the customer.

The original price stays on the order, with your adjustment listed under it. If
anyone asks later why a ₦10,000 sale only brought in ₦8,000, the answer is right
there.

## What about a payment you recorded wrongly?

A payment is not edited either. If you typed ₦5,000 instead of ₦500, you
reverse that payment and record the right one. The reversal stays in the order
history, and the balance goes back to what it should be.

## When the whole sale should not exist: cancel

If the sale never really happened, cancel it. Zalyx asks for a reason and how
the customer was refunded, then reverses every payment once and puts any stock
back. The order stays in your history marked as cancelled.

## The short version

- Wrong item, quantity or price, and not fully paid: **Edit order**.
- The customer has paid in full, but the amount needs to change: **Adjust
  price**.
- A payment was entered wrongly: **reverse the payment**, then record the right
  one.
- The sale should not exist at all: **Cancel order**.

The reason for two tools is simple. Editing keeps an open order tidy. Adjusting
keeps a closed order honest. Together they mean your records match what
actually happened, and you can always see how you got there.

You do not have to remember any of this. If an order is fully paid, Zalyx only
offers **Adjust price** and **Cancel order**, and points you to the right one.
[See how to cancel or correct a sale](/help/cancel-correct-a-sale).
