- **Log in with your fingerprint or face.** Turn it on once after you log in
  and skip typing your PIN next time. It is optional, and your PIN always still
  works.
- **Track your stock.** Tell Zalyx how many you have. It counts down as you
  sell, warns you when you are running low, and adds the stock back if you
  cancel a sale. Reports now show your top-selling products.
- **Customers without a phone number.** Save a customer with just a name, for
  example a child whose parent keeps the phone, or a regular who has none.
- **Record a sale or expense for an earlier day.** Forgot to write it down
  yesterday? Pick the date it really happened, up to 30 days back, and your
  reports count it on the right day.
- **Export records shows what you sold.** Each order in your exported statement
  now lists its items, not just the amount.
- **Edited orders stay correct.** Changing an unpaid order, such as removing an
  item, now keeps the right total and balance after the app syncs.
- **Cancelling refunds each payment once.** Cancelling an order now asks for a
  reason and how the customer was refunded, and never refunds the same payment
  twice.
- **Chat with Zalyx.** Message us on WhatsApp for help, bugs or ideas, straight
  from the More tab. It replaces the WhatsApp Community link.
