- **Inventory.** For things you buy to make or run your business, such as rice,
  fabric or fuel. Record what you buy, count what is left on the shelf, and see
  what you used and what it cost each month. Owners only.
- **Cost price and profit.** Add what a product costs you, and Reports show the
  profit on what you sold.
- **Share an order link.** Send your customer a link to follow their order,
  from the order's menu. No app or sign-in needed on their side.
- **Add or remove stock in one tap.** On a product's screen, add new stock or
  take some away without working out the new total yourself.
- **Find older orders and customers.** Search now also looks beyond what is on
  your phone, and a customer's page shows all their orders.
- **Faster first login.** On a new phone, you can start working while the rest
  of your business loads in the background.
- **Reports tell you when numbers may be out of date,** with a button to
  refresh.
- **Fixes.** Going back after saving a customer no longer reopens the form,
  phone numbers are saved the same way everywhere, and sync is faster and more
  reliable.
