Customers rarely pay everything at once. Zalyx is built around that — you
record what came in today, and the balance takes care of itself.

## At the time of sale

1. When creating the order, set **Paid** to **Partial**.
2. Enter the amount actually paid.
3. Tap **Create Order**. The difference becomes a balance against that
   customer.

## On an order that's already open

1. Open the order.
2. Tap **Record Balance Payment**.
3. Enter the amount paid now.
4. Save it.

The balance updates immediately, and the order's status moves from
**Unpaid** to **Partial**, then to **Paid** once nothing is left
outstanding. Every part payment is kept in the order's history, so you can
always see when each amount came in.
