A walk-in is a customer whose details you don't keep — someone buying once, in
passing, with no reason to save their contact. You still record the sale so
your day's totals stay accurate.

## Record it

1. Open the **Order** tab and tap **Create Order**.
2. Leave the customer field empty, or tap **Walk-in**.
3. Add what they bought — from your catalogue, or typed in directly.
4. Set how much was paid: **Full**, **Partial**, or **Unpaid**.
5. Tap **Create Order**.

No name, no phone number, no problem — the amount is what matters for your
books.

## If they still owe something

A walk-in sale can carry a balance the same way a named customer's can. The
order shows up in your daily report either way, but without a name attached
there's no one to send a reminder to — so a walk-in works best for sales that
are paid in full, or where you're comfortable the balance might go
uncollected.
