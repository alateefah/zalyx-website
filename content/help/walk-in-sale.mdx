A walk-in is a customer whose details you don't keep — someone buying once,
in passing, with no reason to save their contact. You still record the sale
so your day's totals stay accurate.

## Record it

1. Open the **Order** tab and tap **Create Order**.
2. Leave the customer field empty — that's what tells Zalyx it's a walk-in.
3. Add what they bought — from your catalog, or typed in directly.
4. Tap **Create Order**.

No name, no phone number, no problem — the amount is what matters for your
books.

## Why it must be paid in full

A walk-in sale has to be recorded as fully paid. With no name or number
attached, there's no one to chase for the rest if it's left as a balance —
so Zalyx doesn't let a walk-in go out as partial or unpaid. If a sale needs
to carry a balance, save the customer's details first.
