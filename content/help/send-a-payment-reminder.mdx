Chasing money is easier when it's a message instead of a conversation. Zalyx
puts the numbers together for you so the reminder is just a tap away.

## Send one

1. Open the **Customers** tab and tap the customer with a balance.
2. Tap **Send reminder**.
3. Zalyx fills in what they owe and since when — review it, then choose to
   send by WhatsApp or SMS.

## What it says

The reminder states the outstanding balance and a polite request to settle
it — no guesswork, since the amount comes straight from their order history.
You can send a reminder as often as makes sense; it doesn't change the
balance itself, only a part payment or full settlement does.

This only works for named, saved customers — a walk-in sale has no contact
to send a reminder to, which is one more reason to save a customer's details
when you can.
