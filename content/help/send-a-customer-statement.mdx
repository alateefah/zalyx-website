Sometimes a customer just wants to see the numbers for themselves — every
order, every payment, and what's left. A statement lays it out without you
having to explain it over the phone.

## Send one

1. Open the **Customers** tab and tap the customer.
2. Tap **Statement**.
3. Choose a date range, or leave it as their full history.
4. Tap **Share** and send it by WhatsApp, or export it as a PDF.

A statement is read-only — sharing it doesn't change anything on your side.
It's built from the same order and payment records as everywhere else in the
app, so it always matches what you'd see if you looked the customer up
yourself.
