At the end of a trading day, you shouldn't have to add anything up by hand.
The daily report already has.

## Find it

1. Open **More → Reports**.
2. The current day is shown by default — tap a date to see a different
   one, or a range to see several days together.

## What's on it

- **Total Revenue** — the total of every order recorded in the period.
- **Cash Collected** — what customers actually paid you.
- **Expenses** — everything logged against the same period.
- **Estimated Profit** — cash collected minus expenses.
- **Outstanding** — how much of what you're owed is still unpaid.

The report only reflects what's been recorded, so a sale or expense you
forgot to log won't show up until you add it — even after the fact, for the
date it happened.
