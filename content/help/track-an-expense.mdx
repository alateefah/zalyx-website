Sales tell you what came in. Without expenses, that's only half the picture
— what you actually made is sales minus everything that went out to earn
them.

## Log one

1. Open the **Expenses** tab and tap **Add expense**.
2. Enter what it was for and the amount.
3. Pick a category — transport, materials, rent, or your own — so
   similar expenses group together in your reports.
4. Tap **Save Expenses**.

## Where it shows up

Every expense you log is subtracted from your sales in the daily report, so
the profit figure you see is real, not just money in the door. Categorising
expenses consistently also means you can see, over time, where the bulk of
your costs actually go.
