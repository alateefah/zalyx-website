Stock counts drift — a delivery arrives, something gets damaged, or a count
by hand turns up a different number than what's in the app. You can correct
it directly rather than recording fake sales to force the number down.

## Adjust a quantity

1. Open the **Catalogue** tab and tap the product.
2. Tap **Adjust stock**.
3. Enter the new quantity on hand, or add/remove a specific number with a
   short reason (restock, damage, correction).
4. Tap **Save**.

Every sale you record for a product already reduces its stock automatically
— this screen is only for the cases a sale doesn't cover, like a delivery
coming in or a count that doesn't match. Services don't carry a stock
quantity, so there's nothing to adjust there.
