Typing in the same item and price every time you make a sale gets old fast.
Saving it once means every future sale is a couple of taps.

## Add an item

1. Open **More → Products** and tap **Add Product/Service**.
2. Enter a name and a price.
3. Choose whether it's a **Product** or a **Service**.
4. Add a photo if you have one — it's optional, but it's also what shows on
   your storefront if you turn on Zalyx Marketplace.
5. Tap **Save**.

## Using it in a sale

When recording an order, tap **Add from catalog** instead of typing the item
in — pick it from the list, and the price fills in automatically. You can
still adjust the price for that one sale without changing what it's saved
at.
