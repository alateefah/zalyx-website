Typing in the same item and price every time you make a sale gets old fast.
Saving it once means every future sale is a couple of taps.

## Add an item

1. Open the **Catalogue** tab and tap **Add item**.
2. Enter a name and a price.
3. If it's a physical product you keep stock of, add the quantity you have
   on hand. Services can skip this — there's nothing to run out of.
4. Tap **Save**.

## Using it in a sale

When recording an order, tap **Add from catalogue** instead of typing the
item in — pick it from the list, and the price fills in automatically. You
can still adjust the price for that one sale without changing what it's
saved at.

Products and services you add here are also what customers see on your
storefront if you turn on Zalyx Marketplace, so a clear name and a real price
does double duty.
