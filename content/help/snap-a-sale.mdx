Already wrote your sales down on paper? You don't have to type them in a
second time. Snap a photo, and Zalyx reads the page for you.

## Snap it

1. On the **Order** tab, tap the camera icon next to the search bar.
2. Tap **Take / Choose Photo**, then take a photo of your sales log book page
   or invoice, or choose one from your library.
3. Zalyx reads the page and lists what it found.

## Review before you save

Every line is editable before it becomes a sale:

- Choose **One customer** if the whole page was one sale, or **Multiple** if
  each line is a separate sale.
- Add a customer to a line, or leave it as a walk-in.
- Check the item, price, and payment status Zalyx picked up.
- Set a due date for anything still owed.

Tap **Record Sale** (or **Record N Sales** in Multiple mode) once it looks
right.

## Walk-ins still need to be paid in full

Same rule as recording a sale by hand: a line with no customer attached must
be fully paid, since there's no one to chase for a balance. If a sale needs
to carry a balance, add the customer's details to that line first. See
[walk-in sale](/help/walk-in-sale).

## Not available for every business

Snap a sale is built for reading a sales log or invoice, so it's not shown on
the Order tab for mechanic or dispatch businesses.
