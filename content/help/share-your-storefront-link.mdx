Being discoverable on the marketplace helps with new customers — but for
people who already know you, a direct link gets them there faster.

## Get your link

1. Open **More → Edit Business**.
2. Tap **Share storefront**.
3. Share the link directly to WhatsApp, or through whatever else your phone
   offers.

Put it in your WhatsApp status, a flyer, or a social media bio — anywhere a
potential customer might already be looking.
