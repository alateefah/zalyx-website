Being discoverable on the marketplace helps with new customers — but for
people who already know you, a direct link gets them there faster.

## Get your link

1. Open **More → Storefront**.
2. Tap **Share storefront**.
3. Copy the link, or share it directly to WhatsApp, SMS, or anywhere else.

The link works the same whether or not your storefront meets all four
discoverability conditions — someone with the direct link can always see it,
even while you're still finishing setup. It only stops working if you turn
your storefront off entirely.

Put it in your WhatsApp status, a flyer, or a social media bio — anywhere a
potential customer might already be looking.
