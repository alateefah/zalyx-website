If someone else records sales or takes payments for you, they should have
their own account — not your phone and your password.

## Invite one

1. Open **More → Manage Staff**.
2. Tap **Invite staff** and enter their name and phone number.
3. Zalyx creates an invite code and link — share it with them directly, or
   send it over WhatsApp.
4. They open the link to join your business under their own login.

The invite expires after 7 days if it isn't used, so send it while it's
fresh. Every sale a staff member records afterward is attributed to them, so
you can always see who did what without asking.
