If someone else records sales or takes payments for you, they should have
their own account — not your phone and your password.

## Add one

1. Open **More → Staff**.
2. Tap **Add staff**.
3. Enter their name and phone number.
4. Choose what they can do — record sales, view reports, manage the
   catalogue — and save.
5. They'll receive an invite to set up their own login.

## Why it's worth doing

Every sale a staff member records is attributed to them, so you can see who
did what without asking. And because their access is limited to what you
chose, they can do their job without seeing everything you can — your full
reports, for instance, can stay yours alone.
