A storefront is only useful if a customer can talk to you after finding it —
Zalyx doesn't build a new inbox for that, it hands them straight to
WhatsApp.

## Set it

1. Open **More → Storefront**.
2. Tap **WhatsApp number**.
3. Enter the number customers should message — it doesn't have to be the
   same number your account is registered with.
4. Tap **Save**.

Once it's set, every visitor to your storefront gets a **Message on
WhatsApp** button that opens a chat with that number directly. Until it's
set, that button stays disabled and your storefront won't appear in the
marketplace — a customer who finds you but can't reach you does more harm
than not being found at all.
