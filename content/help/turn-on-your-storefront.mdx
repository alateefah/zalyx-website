Your storefront is part of Zalyx Ledger, not a separate app — the products
you already record are what customers see once it's visible. It's on by
default for every business, so there's usually nothing to switch on; what's
worth checking is whether you meet the conditions to actually appear.

## Check your storefront settings

1. Open **More → Edit Business**.
2. Scroll to the marketplace section, where the storefront toggle and your
   city, WhatsApp number and description live together.

Four things need to be true before your storefront actually appears on the
marketplace:

- Your storefront is on.
- You've set a WhatsApp number.
- Your city is set.
- You have a description, or at least one product listed.

The app shows you exactly which of these are still missing. Once all four
are in place, your storefront goes live automatically — there's no separate
review or approval step.
