Your storefront is part of Zalyx Ledger, not a separate app — the products
you already record are what customers see once it's on.

## Turn it on

1. Open **More → Storefront**.
2. Tap the toggle to turn your storefront on.

That alone doesn't make you discoverable yet. Four things need to be true
before your storefront actually appears on the marketplace:

- Your storefront is turned on.
- You've set a WhatsApp number.
- Your city is set.
- You have a description, or at least one product listed.

The app shows you exactly which of these are still missing. Once all four
are in place, your storefront goes live automatically — there's no separate
review or approval step.
