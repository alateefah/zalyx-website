Mistakes happen — a wrong amount typed in, the wrong item picked, or an order
that shouldn't have been created at all. You don't have to live with it.

## Correct an order

1. Open the **Order** tab and find the order.
2. Tap it, then tap **Edit**.
3. Change the item, amount, or payment status.
4. Tap **Save**. Your reports and any balance owed update immediately.

## Cancel an order

1. Open the order and tap **Cancel order**.
2. Confirm. The order is removed from your totals, and any balance it
   created against a customer is cleared.

A cancelled order still appears in your history marked as cancelled, so
there's a record of what happened — it just no longer counts toward what you
sold or what's owed.
