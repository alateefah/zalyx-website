Selling to someone you've dealt with before? Attaching the sale to their
customer record keeps every order, payment and balance in one place instead
of scattered across separate walk-in entries.

## Record it

1. Open the **Order** tab and tap **Create Order**.
2. Tap the customer field and search their name or phone number.
3. If they're new, tap **Add customer** and save their name and phone number
   first — it takes a few seconds and pays off the next time they buy.
4. Add what they bought and set how much was paid.
5. Tap **Create Order**.

## Why bother saving them

Once a customer is saved, every sale you record for them adds to one running
history. You can see everything they've bought, everything they owe, and
send them a payment reminder by name — none of which is possible for a
walk-in sale with no one attached.
